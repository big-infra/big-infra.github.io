Economic Value Calculator

Required premium ρ, benchmark rEr, loss Δrr, capital intensity CI and economic value E. Per-sqm values; rates in %.

Inputs

M = L + D and rp = (Y + gL − dD)/(L + D). Defaults: the Karad plot (₹1,50,000 rent on 500 sqm).

Results

CI vs ρ — linear

E vs ρ — hyperbola

Δrr vs ρ — linear

E vs rp — linear

Benchmark scenarios

ρ = i is a calibration (FD rate plus a premium equal to inflation), not something inflation implies; support it with regional data. E assumes the real return flow Q = M·rpr is held fixed, and CI and E need rpr > 0 and rEr > 0.
