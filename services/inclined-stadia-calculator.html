    Formulas used

    With staff vertical, vertical angle θ, staff intercept s, constants k and C:

    L = k·s·cosθ + C (inclined distance)
    H = k·s·cos²θ + C·cosθ (horizontal distance)
    V = k·s·sinθ·cosθ + C·sinθ (vertical component, negative for depression)
    RL of staff point = station RL + HI + V − h, where h is the axial reading.

    When you enter the inclined distance directly, H = L·cosθ and V = L·sinθ. A zenith angle z is converted with θ = 90° − z.
