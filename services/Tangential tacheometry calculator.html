    Formulas used

    Angles are signed: elevation is positive, depression is negative, and a zenith angle z becomes 90° − z. θu is the angle to the upper vane and θl the angle to the lower vane, with the staff vertical and the vanes a distance s apart.

    D = s / (tanθu − tanθl) (horizontal distance)
    Vl = D·tanθl and Vu = D·tanθu (vertical components to each vane)
    RL of staff point = station RL + HI + Vl − hl, where hl is the staff reading at the lower vane.

    With vane readings, s = hu − hl. This works for two elevations, two depressions, or one of each.
