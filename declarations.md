  <h2>About Using Trademark Symbol</h2>
  <p>As informed by the legal advisor, consultant and expert in IP laws, there is no legal significance to the ™ mark. It simply indicates that there is an application filed for the trademark. This also resolves scenarios where a party who initially applied but whose application is not registered may be treated differently from a party applying later.</p>
  <p>There is no legal binding or obligation for the ™ mark; however, we use it as part of the application process and represent the application ID wherever reasonably appropriate.</p>
